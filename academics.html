Our Academic Approach

Child-Centred Learning

Learning activities are designed according to children's abilities, interests, and developmental stages.

Creative Learning

Children learn through play, storytelling, art, music, and practical hands-on activities.

Holistic Development

We focus on nurturing intellectual, social, emotional, and physical growth balanced together.

Classes Offered

Baby Class

Introduction to foundational learning through interactive play and sensory activities.

Age 2–3 Years

Middle Class

Developing essential language, number sense, creative expression, and social confidence.

Age 3–4 Years

Top Class

Preparing young learners for Primary One transition through structured academic activities.

Age 4–6 Years

Learning Areas

Language

English communication, early phonics, reading, and vocabulary development.

Mathematics

Number recognition, simple counting, shapes, and early problem-solving skills.

Science

Exploring nature, sensory discovery, and understanding the surrounding environment.

Creative Arts

Drawing, painting, music, dance, and encouraging imaginative self-expression.
