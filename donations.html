Support Our Children

Every contribution you make helps provide quality education, healthy meals, learning materials and a better future for our children.

Why Donate?

Kid's Love Nursery & Daycare Centre serves children from different backgrounds. Your generous support helps us improve learning facilities, provide scholastic materials, meals, and create a safe environment where every child can learn and grow.

What Your Donation Supports

School Fees

Support children from vulnerable families to access quality education.

Learning Materials

Provide books, toys, uniforms, scholastic materials and classroom supplies.

Meals

Help provide nutritious meals that keep our children healthy and ready to learn.

Development Projects

Improve classrooms, playgrounds, sanitation facilities and other school infrastructure.
