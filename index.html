About Our Center

Founded by Mr. and Mrs. Kirinya Herbert Antony | Established 2018

School History & Foundation

Kid’s Love Nursery and Day Care Center was established in 2018 by two passionate directors with a background in education and extensive experience in Uganda’s education system. Located in Nakasozi – Buddo, the center was born out of a desire to bridge quality gaps in early childhood learning, reduce long travel distances for young kids, and provide affordable, loving care to the community.

Vision, Mission & Philosophy

School Vision: Developing a God-fearing pupil equipped with Quality education for the development of the nation.


School Mission: To provide a holistic kind of education.


School Philosophy: We believe that happy, loved, and connected kids are destined for success in every facet of their lives.

Management Structure

The school is overseen by an independent Board of 5 Governors and Managed by Directors who serve as school agents.
