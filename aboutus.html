About Our School

Kid's Love Nursery & Pre-primary School is committed to providing quality early childhood education in a safe, caring and Christian environment. We nurture every learner's intellectual, moral, social, emotional and physical development while helping each child discover and develop their unique talents.

Our Story

Our Journey Since 2018

Kid's Love Nursery & Pre-primary School was established in 2018 with a vision of providing affordable, quality and holistic early childhood education to children in Nakasozi, Buddo and neighbouring communities.

Since its establishment, the school has continued to grow through God's grace, committed teachers, supportive parents and hardworking learners.

We have built a child-friendly learning environment that promotes creativity, confidence, discipline, innovation and excellence in every learner.

Today, our school continues to prepare children for primary education while nurturing strong Christian values and lifelong learning skills.
