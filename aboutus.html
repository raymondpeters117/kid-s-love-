School History & Foundation

Kid’s Love Nursery and Daycare Centre was established in 2018 by passionate directors with experience in Uganda’s education system.

Located in Nakasozi – Buddo, the centre was founded to provide quality early childhood education, reduce long travelling distances for young children and provide affordable, loving care to families.

Vision, Mission & Philosophy

School Vision:
Developing a God-fearing pupil equipped with quality education for the development of the nation.

School Mission:
To provide a holistic kind of education.

School Motto:
Carry your candle and light the world.

School Philosophy:
We believe that happy, loved and connected children are destined for success in every area of life.

Management Structure

The school is overseen by an independent Board of 5 Governors and managed by Directors who serve as school agents.
