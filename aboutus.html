About Our School

Kid's Love Nursery & Pre-primary School is dedicated to providing quality early childhood education in a safe, loving and Christian environment where every child is given the opportunity to discover and develop his or her unique talents.

Our Story

Our Journey Since 2018

Kid's Love Nursery & Pre-primary School was established in 2018 with a vision of providing quality, affordable and holistic early childhood education to children within Nakasozi, Buddo and the surrounding communities.

The school was founded by passionate educationists who recognized the need for a nurturing environment where young learners could develop academically, socially, emotionally and spiritually.

Since its establishment, the school has continued to grow through God's grace, dedicated teachers, supportive parents and hardworking learners.

Today, Kid's Love Nursery proudly provides a safe, clean and child-friendly environment that promotes creativity, confidence and excellence.
