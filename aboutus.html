About Our Center

Founded by Mr. and Mrs. Kirinya Herbert Antony | Established 2018

School History & Foundation

Kid’s Love Nursery and Day Care Center was established in 2018 by two passionate directors with a background in education and extensive experience in Uganda’s education system. Located in Nakasozi – Buddo, the center was founded with a desire to provide quality early childhood learning, reduce long travel distances for young children, and offer affordable, loving care to the community.

Vision, Mission & Philosophy

School Vision: Developing a God-fearing pupil equipped with quality education for the development of the nation.

School Mission: To provide a holistic kind of education.

School Motto: Carry your candle and light the world.

School Philosophy: We believe that happy, loved, and connected children are destined for success in every facet of their lives.

Management Structure

The school is overseen by an independent Board of 5 Governors and managed by Directors who serve as school agents.
